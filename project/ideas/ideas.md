## New Features
- the objective is to keep the season interesting.  Currently, once you fall behind, it can be tough to catch up
- so a way to
    - add more points as bonus
    - earn "rewards" (stickers!) during the season

- mini-games during the season
- ways to get bonus points to allow folks the chance to catch up as the season progresses
- doubling confidence
    - "every week, you could make one of your confidence points double, but you could only double each level once in the season. That would mean one week there would be no double, but one week, you would double the 1, one week the 2, etc. You would pick which week for each one, but would also double the impact of missing on a 16 doubled.
- head to head challenge
    - during the season, you can challenge someone
    - that week, if you get more points, you earn a bonus
    - But if you lose, the challenger gets the bonus and you lose it
    - no risk be being the challenged
    - you can only challenge each person once

- auto-pick button
    - can choose to pick all home teams and randome points
    - other method?

- international games bonus

- stickers
    - most visitor teams picked
