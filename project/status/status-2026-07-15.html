Current Truth

The app is live at geeks-pickem.replit.app with the full 2026 schedule (272 games), working Replit Auth login, and a wired pick → score → standings path — but none of the core flows have been verified end-to-end with real users and real data. A full code review today found six high-priority issues, including a membership hole (anyone with a Replit account can join) and a dropped-weeks bug that will zero everyone's standings for the first month of the season. On the league-ops side, a beta email went to 15 friends on June 10 and only one friend sign-in is confirmed; the invite email list now exists (17 addresses in the July diary) but hasn't been seeded. The next proof point is a verified Week 1 workflow behind an invite-only gate — and the deadline is 31 days away.
