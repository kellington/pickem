Key Decisions (5 Most Recent)

2026-05-15
SameSite=None session cookie for Replit iframe context
The Replit preview embeds the app in an iframe; default SameSite=Lax withholds the cookie on cross-site API calls, breaking auth after login.
2026-05-15
Auto-create league member on profile setup
Anyone who can reach the app URL is trusted for v1; pre-seeding approved records before first login creates unnecessary friction for a private friend group.
2026-05-15
Four dropped regular-season weeks
League wants Yahoo-style dropped weeks; configurable per season via dropped_week_count on the seasons row, initially 4.
2026-05-15
Per-game pick reveal (not whole-week)
A Monday night pick should stay hidden until that game starts — granular per-game reveal is fairer than unlocking the whole week at once.
2026-05-15
Derived standings from pick scores (not a maintained table)
Standings should be reproducible from picks, results, scoring rules, and dropped-week config — not from a manually maintained source-of-truth table.

Feature Ideas — Top 10

1-on-1 Weekly Challenge
Challenge one friend for the week; whoever scores more picks earns the combined points. You can only challenge each person once per season. No penalty for being challenged.
communication ideas.md Engagement
Double Confidence
Once per season per confidence level, you can double a pick's value. You choose which week to double each level — high risk/reward since a miss also doubles.
ideas.md Rules
Bonus Week (32-point game)
Designated bonus weeks where one game is worth 32 confidence points — a chance for trailing players to close the gap in a single swing.
communication Engagement
International Game Bonus
Games played at international neutral sites (London, Munich, Melbourne) are worth 5× the normal confidence points to reward attention to unique matchups.
communication ideas.md Rules
Top Weekly Score Multiplier
The highest-confidence game pick of the week (if correct) is worth 3× — rewards bold picks and adds a weekly highlight moment.
communication Engagement
Auto-pick Button
One-click to auto-assign picks by Vegas odds or randomly for players who miss the deadline or want a quick fill. Configurable method (odds vs random).
communication ideas.md UX
Stickers & Achievements
Earn stickers for weekly achievements — most visitor teams picked, perfect week, upset picker, etc. Displayed on player profiles as seasonal badges.
ideas.md Engagement
Catch-up Bonus Points
Trailing players get access to bonus point opportunities as the season progresses — keeps the competition interesting for everyone past Week 4.
ideas.md Engagement
Playoff Bracket Phase
Separate post-regular-season playoff competition with its own bracket format and scoring rules, distinct from the regular-season standings.
ideas.md Rules
Display-only Odds / Spreads
Show Vegas odds or spread information alongside each matchup as context — display only, never affects scoring. Helps with informed confidence assignments.
