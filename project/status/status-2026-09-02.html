Current Truth

The app is live at geeks-pickem.replit.app with working Replit Auth, the full 2026 schedule (272 games), and the main pick, group-picks, scoring, and standings surfaces. The September work added odds guidance, Favorites/Upsets/Random helpers, completion messaging, transferable confidence points, and aggregate team-pick statistics; the confidence selector was refined again on September 2. The uncomfortable truth is that the core Week 1 rehearsal is still not proven end-to-end with a second user, production results, and scoring, and the invite-only gate and automated tests remain unfinished. There are 10 active members and 16 invited rows, including 7 likely stale or duplicate invitations, while Week 1 starts September 9 — so the next move is verification and roster cleanup, not another feature.
